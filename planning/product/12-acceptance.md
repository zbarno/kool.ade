**Definition of done (BINDING — D-20 as ratified by D-21).** The planner's suggested form was confirmed by the operator without amendment (CLR-007 resolved). The MVP is done when BOTH legs pass. The bar below is alterable only by fresh ruling (anti-drift discipline, cf. D-19) — not by quiet implication, not by erosion.

1. *Fixture-repo demonstration (the centerpiece).* A small, prepared, self-contained git fixture repository with naive seeded planning state, exercised through one live multi-turn interview. Passing requires an outcome checklist observed in-session and subsequently legible in the git history: (a) the specification visibly improves on repository insight — sections earned, wrong claims corrected, code-grounded findings cited; (b) items arise in at least two distinct categories with sensible assignments; (c) ownership-gap synthesis fires for at least one unowned category (FR-7) — NOTE FOR THE DEMONSTRATION RUNNER: D-25 left this repository with no unowned lane, so outcome (c) must be demonstrated in the FIXTURE repository's own configuration (seed it with at least one unowned category), not in the planner's home tree; (d) at least one answer becomes a recorded decision with its item cleared, reflected in the spec; (e) the checkpoint chain is intact with imperative subjects (FR-3, NFR-9); (f) a deliberately invalid or misrouted envelope proves zero mutation — prior artifacts byte-identical, problems surfaced (F-6, FR-4); (g) cancelling an in-flight turn discards fragments cleanly (FR-5). The demonstration is scripted in the runbook sense (fixed steps) but plays live against the real model, since the §25 wager is precisely on real conversational dynamics. Scratch-repo preparation follows the codebase's own minimal-dependency discipline (the tests already hand-roll temp directories rather than pulling a `tempfile` crate); the landed fixture preparer (ticket 005, `examples/prepare_exit_demo.rs`) and its runbook (`docs/exit-demo-runbook.md`) are the instrumented vehicle.
