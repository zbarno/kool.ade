## 1. Vision

The central product experiment (Contract §25): can an external AI agent harness, driven continuously, turn a loosely defined project idea into a steadily better specification by finding the most important unanswered questions, routing them to the right people, and recording the answers?

- G-1 Conversation is the interface: the interview produces the specification, and the specification never drifts from agreed content.
- G-2 Uncertainty never dies quietly: every gap becomes a typed, prioritized, category-routed open item.
- G-3 Git is the storage system: history, diffs, rollback, and collaboration trail come for free (Contract §2); D-18 extends this — git remains the durable substrate, and the intended real-time peer collaboration rides a transient overlay on top of it (phased post-MVP by D-22).
- G-4 Clean harness seam: the app orchestrates; the pi CLI performs all LLM interaction (Contract §14–15).
