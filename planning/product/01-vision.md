## 1. Vision

Packet is a git-backed planning partner for long-lived products. It investigates repositories, turns accepted intent into durable specifications and implementation tasks, and shows actionable uncertainty on a Kanban board.

- G-1 Conversation is the operator interface; durable conclusions move into project artifacts rather than depending on old chat context.
- G-2 Uncertainty remains explicit until resolved. Packet handles evidence-based work autonomously and asks the operator only for a blocking human decision.
- G-3 Git holds shared product truth, feature history, task snapshots, decisions, and audit history. A future real-time channel may be transient over git (D-18, D-22).
- G-4 Packet orchestrates all planning and coding through the external Pi harness; provider inference is outside the application.

The central hypothesis remains that a continuously operating agent can improve a product specification by investigating the code, locating consequential gaps, and recording accepted answers.
