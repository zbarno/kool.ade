## CLR-015

**Priority:** Normal
**Type:** Question
**Category:** Development
**Assigned To:** Development
**Status:** Open

### Question
Channel topology and trust: direct app-to-app WebSocket (which instance listens, and how do peers discover each other — LAN scan, manually entered endpoint, git-remote metadata?), or a shared relay process/service (adding a hosted component beyond the D-11 from-source-on-your-own-workstation framing)? Local-network only, or crossing the internet? And how do peers authenticate given the no-accounts posture — signing over the git-derived identity?


### Reason
Deprioritized by D-22's post-MVP phasing ruling (CLR-014 resolved): this no longer gates MVP planning or the §12 exit bar, and is now a leading design question of the post-MVP channel project proper — it decides what Packet becomes network-wise (a listener, a dialer, or a relay), scopes the NFR-5 attack surface before the channel ships, fixes the transport-library and runtime choice against NFR-7's offline/minimal posture, and determines whether off-site teammates are reachable at all.
